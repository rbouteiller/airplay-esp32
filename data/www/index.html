Playback

What AirPlay, Bluetooth and Sendspin show. It changes on the network after a restart.

Some software only works with AirPlay v1. Apple devices still work in that mode, but without v2 features such as multi-speaker sync. The volume and track buttons on the speaker need v1. Takes effect after a restart.

Experimental. Lets a Sendspin server such as Music Assistant play to this speaker. Takes effect after a restart.

AirPlay and Bluetooth take priority: starting a session there pauses the Sendspin server's queue and takes the output, and ending it hands the output back and resumes.
